# The New Lunar Accord (NLA)
**International Treaty for Diplomatic Zoning, Sustainable Development, and Coexistence on Earth's Moon**

*Conducted under the auspices of the United Nations Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS) and the Five Founding Signatory Powers.*

---

## Preamble

**WE, THE PEOPLE OF PLANET EARTH**, recognizing that the expansion of human civilization into space represents a monumental milestone in our shared history, and acknowledging the cultural, symbolic, and scientific inspiration drawn from our collective narratives and pop culture;

**DESIRING** to prevent geopolitical conflict, resource militarization, and territorial dispute beyond Earth's atmosphere;

**DETERMINED** to replace the former paradigm of unilateral claim with a framework of diplomatic sector governance, shared technological progress, and ecological stewardship;

**DO HEREBY ESTABLISH** the **New Lunar Accord (NLA)** as the supreme regulatory framework governing all human activities, bases, industrial ventures, and biological preserves on the Moon.

---

## Article I: The Diplomatic Balance of Powers

To ensure global equilibrium and foster strategic cooperation, the lunar surface is divided into five sovereign operational sectors aligned into two major philosophical blocks:

### Section 1. The Galactic Republic Block (East Hemisphere / Jedi Alignment)
Focusing on civil governance, open commerce, deep-space observation, and orbital infrastructure.

1. **United States Sector (US-NLA):**
   * **Location:** East Visible Hemisphere (*Mare Tranquillitatis*, *Mare Serenitatis*).
   * **Primary Mandate:** Heavy payload transport logistics, orbital communication networks, commercial spaceports, and civil administration.

### Section 2. The Galactic Empire Block (West & Far Side / Sith Alignment)
Focusing on heavy industry, energy harvesting, extreme environment research, and life-support biology.

2. **China Sector (CN-NLA):**
   * **Location:** Far Side of the Moon (*South Pole-Aitken Basin*, *Von Kármán Crater*).
   * **Primary Mandate:** Helium-3 extraction, nuclear fusion energy generation, and autonomous robotic manufacturing.
3. **Russia Sector (RU-NLA):**
   * **Location:** North Polar Region (*Peary Crater*, *Hermite Crater*).
   * **Primary Mandate:** Deep regolith extraction, heavy structural engineering, and ice/water processing.
4. **Brazil Sector (BR-NLA):**
   * **Location:** Equatorial & South Polar Transit Corridors (*Sinus Iridii*, *Schackleton Rim Bio-reserve*).
   * **Primary Mandate:** Closed-loop biosphere systems, agro-space engineering, hydroponic food production, life-support logistics, and ecological stewardship.

## Article II: The Brazilian Sector & The Tree of Life

### Section 1. Functional Responsibility
The Brazilian Sector holds exclusive administrative jurisdiction over the **Global Lunar Life Support Infrastructure (GLLSI)**. It is responsible for supplying organic food, purified oxygen ($O_2$), and recycled water loops to all signatory bases.

### Section 2. The Insignia of the Tree of Life
1. The official seal and flag of the Brazilian Sector shall feature the **Tree of Life** (*Árvore da Vida*).
2. The emblem represents the transfer of Earth’s biodiversity to space, environmental renewal, and the foundational role of organic life amidst the vacuum of space.
3. All agricultural modules, bio-domes, and life-support shipments originating from the sector shall bear this mark.

---

## Article III: Non-Aggression & Emergency Coexistence

1. **Demilitarization:** No nuclear weapons, orbital kinetic bombardment systems, or offensive directed-energy weapons shall be deployed in lunar orbit or on the surface.
2. **Universal Assistance Protocol:** In the event of depressurization, radiation storms, or life-support failure, any base belonging to any signatory power MUST open its airlocks and provide immediate refuge to personnel regardless of national affiliation.
3. **Open Airspaces:** Transiting shuttle vehicles carrying humanitarian supplies, fresh crops, or emergency water are granted unrestricted access through all sector airspaces.

---

## Article IV: Scientific & Technological Open Standard

1. **Interoperability:** All airlocks, docking rings, power grids, and communication protocols must adhere to open, universal standards.
2. **Resource Sharing:** Scientific telemetry, seismic data, and solar flare warning logs generated by any sector must be broadcasted publicly in real-time.
