### Key Technical Subsystems:
* **Transpiration Condensers:** Dehumidifiers inside the Bio-Domes capture water vapor transpired by plant foliage, redirecting clean condensate directly back into nutrient reservoirs.
* **Sub-Surface Ice Melting:** Polar meltwater from Shackleton Crater rim ice is purified using high-pressure Reverse Osmosis (RO) before initial injection into the loop.

## 4. Bio-Fuel & Bio-Propellant Production

To reduce dependence on Earth-supplied propellants, the Brazilian Sector utilizes agricultural waste (crop residue, cellulose, and excess algae biomass) to produce bio-methane ($CH_4$) and bio-ethanol ($C_2H_5OH$) for lunar hopping vehicles and thermal power generation.

### 4.1 Anaerobic Digestion (Bio-Methane)
1. Non-edible crop biomass (stalks, leaves, roots) is pre-treated with enzyme baths to break down lignin.
2. Methanogenic archaea digest biomass in sealed thermophilic digesters at 55°C.
3. Biogas ($65\%\ CH_4$, $35\%\ CO_2$) is scrubbed. The methane is cryo-cooled into **Liquid Methane ($LCH_4$)**.

### 4.2 Bio-Ethanol Fermentation
1. Starch-rich residual cassava pulp is converted to fermentable sugars via alpha-amylase enzymes.
2. Specialized yeast strains (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*) ferment the mash into ethanol under zero-headspace bioreactors.
3. High-efficiency vacuum distillation yields **99.5% pure anhydrous ethanol**, used as a clean combustion fuel and chemical feedstock.
