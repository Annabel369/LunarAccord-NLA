# Standard Emergency & Safety Protocols (NLA-SEP-2026)
**Joint Multi-Sector Operational Directives for Base Contingencies, Radiation Events, and Life-Support Failures**

*Ratified under Article III of the New Lunar Accord (NLA) — Inter-Sector Safety Commission*

---

## 1. Universal Emergency Principles

Under Article III of the **New Lunar Accord**, all signatory bases (US, CN, RU, BR) operate under the **Mandatory Universal Refuge Policy**. In any life-threatening emergency, national boundaries and sector restrictions are immediately suspended. Airspaces, automated airlocks, and medical bays must open automatically to assist any personnel in distress, regardless of national affiliation.

---

## 2. Primary Contingency Protocols

### Protocol 2.1: Solar Particle Events (SPE) & Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR)
Solar flares and high-energy coronal mass ejections present lethal radiation hazards requiring immediate protocol activation upon orbital detection.

1. **Alert Classifications:**
   * **YELLOW (M-Class Flare):** Surface operations restricted to 30-minute radii. Secondary radiation shielding activated.
   * **RED (X-Class Flare / CME):** Surface Extra-Vehicular Activity (EVA) canceled immediately. All personnel must seek shelter.
2. **Action Plan:**
   * Surface personnel must reach the nearest underground regolith bunker or shielded habitat within **15 minutes**.
   * Bases equipped with **Brazilian Hydro-Shielding Modules** (water and microalgae wall buffers) will unlock emergency shelter sectors for transiting personnel.
   * Automated beacons must broadcast location logs every 30 seconds until the SPE hazard passes.

---

### Protocol 2.2: Habitat Depressurization & Hull Breach
Rapid atmospheric loss from micro-meteorite impacts or structural seal fatigue requires immediate localized containment.

1. **Automated Containment:**
   * Atmospheric pressure sensors detecting levels below **80 kPa** trigger instant automatic bulkhead isolation.
2. **Personal Safety Procedures:**
   * Personnel must don Emergency Quick-Donning Suits (located at 10-meter intervals in all corridors) within **45 seconds**.
3. **Atmospheric Restoration:**
   * Surrounding sectors route emergency nitrogen and oxygen reserves via the **Brazilian Sector GLLSI network** to stabilize pressure in adjacent compartments.

---

### Protocol 2.3: Life-Support System & Scrubbing Failure
If a base experiences critical mechanical failure of its primary $CO_2$ scrubbers or $O_2$ generation systems:

1. **Emergency Rerouting:**
   * The facility links directly to the **Inter-Sector Shared Oxygen Grid** maintained by the nearest Brazilian Sector Bio-Dome.
2. **Evacuation Timeline:**
   * If localized atmospheric restoration cannot be achieved within **2 hours**, personnel are evacuated using pressurized all-terrain rovers to the nearest neighboring base.

---

## 3. Universal Communication & Distress Frequencies

All lunar habitats, rovers, and EVA suits must maintain continuous dual-watch monitoring on standardized NLA emergency bands:

| Channel / Frequency | Designation | Access Authorization |

| **NLA-EMERGENCY 1 (121.50 MHz)** | Universal Distress Signal (SOS) | Unrestricted / Auto-Broadcast |
| **NLA-MEDICAL 2 (243.00 MHz)** | Medical Evacuation & Triage | Inter-Sector Medical Units |
| **NLA-AGRO-NET 3 (433.92 MHz)** | Bio-Dome & Water Grid Alerts | Sector Brazil Operations |

---

## 4. Universal Airlock & Docking Standards

To prevent lockouts during emergency evacuations, all outer airlocks across all five sectors conform to the **NLA Universal Docking & Airlock Specification**:

1. **Mechanical Override:** Every exterior airlock door features a high-visibility, illuminated mechanical override handle operable without digital credentials or power.
2. **Standardized Consumables:** Suit recharge ports for oxygen ($O_2$), power, and coolant fluids are identical across all space suit models (US, CN, RU, BR).
