# Standard Emergency & Safety Protocols
**Joint Multi-Sector Operational Directives for Base Contingencies, Radiation Events, and Habitat Integrity**

*Ratified under Article III of the New Lunar Accord (NLA) — Inter-Sector Safety Commission*

---

## 1. Universal Emergency Principles

Under Article III of the **New Lunar Accord**, all signatory bases (US, CN, RU, BR) operate under the **Mandatory Universal Refuge Policy**. In any life-threatening emergency, national boundaries and sector restrictions are immediately suspended. Airspaces, airlocks, and pressure chambers must open automatically to assist any personnel in distress.

---

## 2. Emergency Response Protocols

### Protocol 2.1: Solar Particle Events (SPE) & Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR)
Solar storms present extreme radiation hazards requiring immediate protocol activation upon detection by orbital telemetry.

1. **Alert Levels:**
   * **YELLOW:** Solar flare detected ($M$-class). Prepare shielding sectors.
   * **RED:** Severe SPE imminent ($X$-class). All surface activity (EVA) suspended immediately.
2. **Action Plan:**
   * Surface personnel must reach the nearest underground regolith bunker or heavily shielded module within **15 minutes**.
   * Bases equipped with **Brazilian Hydro-Shielding Modules** (water/algae wall buffers) will make emergency radiation shelters available for transiting personnel from any sector.
   * Automated communications must broadcast beacon status every 30 seconds.

---

### Protocol 2.2: Habitat Rapid Depressurization
Pressure loss from micro-meteorite impacts or structural seal failures requires immediate localized containment.

1. **Automated Containment:**
   * Pressure sensors below $80\text{ kPa}$ trigger automatic bulkhead doors to seal off the affected compartment.
2. **Life Safety Procedures:**
   * Personnel must immediately don emergency quick-donning pressure suits (located at every bulkhead station).
   * Surrounding sectors are notified automatically to route emergency oxygen reserves from the **Brazilian Sector GLLSI network** to stabilize atmospheric levels in unaffected adjacent modules.

---

### Protocol 2.3: Life Support System Failure ($O_2$ / $CO_2$ Scrubber Malfunction)
If a primary base experiences failure of mechanical air-scrubbing systems:

1. **Atmospheric Rerouting:**
   * The base taps into the **Inter-Sector Shared Oxygen Line** fed by the nearest Brazilian Sector Bio-Dome.
2. **Emergency Evacuation:**
   * If localized atmospheric restoration is impossible within 2 hours, personnel are evacuated to the nearest adjacent base using pressurized ground rovers.

---

## 3. Universal Communication & Distress Signals

All lunar habitats, rovers, and suits must continuously listen on the standardized inter-sector emergency frequency bands:

| Channel / Frequency | Purpose | Access Authorization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NLA-EMERGENCY 1 (121.50 MHz)** | Universal Distress Signal / SOS | Unrestricted / Automatic |
| **NLA-MEDICAL 2 (243.00 MHz)** | Medical Evacuation & Triage | Inter-Sector Medical Teams |
| **NLA-AGRO-NET 3 (433.92 MHz)** | Bio-Dome & Water Infrastructure Alerts | Sector Brazil Operations |

---

## 4. Emergency Airlock Procedures

All outer airlocks across all five sectors must conform to the **NLA Universal Docking & Airlock Specification**:

1. **Manual Override:** Every exterior airlock door features an illuminated physical manual override handle usable without electronic clearance.
2. **Suit Compatibility:** Emergency recharge ports for oxygen ($O_2$) and power supply connectors are standardized across all space suit models (US, CN, RU, BR).
